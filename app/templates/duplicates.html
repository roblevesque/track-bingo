Find duplicates

Songs that look like copies of each other: the same audio file, or the same title by the same artist (ignoring capitals, accents, brackets such as “(Remastered)” and a leading “The”). A live version can look like the studio one, so check before merging or deleting.

Merging keeps one song and folds the others into it: tags are combined, a missing artist, note or play window is filled in from a copy, and every game, card and saved plan that used a copy now uses the kept song. The copies and their audio files are then deleted.
